# Rainbow Triko — Atelier Tycoon (mobile)

Isometric 2.5D knitting-shop tycoon for phones (portrait). Built with Phaser 3, single HTML file, Turkish/English.

**Play:** https://s3tr.github.io/rainbow-triko/

## How to play
- Tap a **wool basket** → tap the **knitting machine** → collect the sweater → stock the **shelf**.
- Customers take the colour in their bubble and queue at the **till** — tap it to sell.
- Taps queue up (max 5); Sevgi does them in order.
- Shop day runs 09:00–19:00 (~4 min). Spend earnings on upgrades each evening.
- The kids arrive at 16:00: Sırrı stocks shelves, Arzu runs machines (day 2+), Gökçen works the till (day 3+).
- Naime may drop by and slow everyone down — tap her to call Mehmet Ali.
